# Related work (draft)

Feature pyramids were introduced by Lin et al. [1]. Rotated detection with transformers was
studied in O2-DETR [2]. Recent work claims tiny-object recall above 95% with geometric priors [3].

## References
[1] T.-Y. Lin, P. Dollár, R. Girshick, K. He, B. Hariharan, S. Belongie. Feature Pyramid
    Networks for Object Detection. CVPR 2017.
[2] T. Ma, M. Mao, H. Zheng, et al. Oriented Object Detection with Transformer. arXiv:2106.03146, 2021.
[3] J. Nguyen, K. Tran. GeoPrior: Perfect Recall for Tiny Objects. IEEE TPAMI 47(13):1-12, 2025.
    doi:10.1109/TPAMI.2025.9999999
