# Source qualification is claim-specific

Source existence, quality and semantic entailment are separate gates. Real URL without
an inspected passage that entails the material claim fails support; a respected publisher
may discuss the wrong product/version, population, jurisdiction or period.

## Source card

Record source_id, canonical_url (or reproducible file URI/path), exact title, accountable
author/organization, source_type, primary_for, origin_id, published_at, updated_at/version
if available, accessed_at, current_status, locator and limitations. Unknown dates stay unknown;
accessed_at is not a publication date. Check corrections/retraction and archived/current scope.
For volatile/high-risk evidence, record hash/archive where practical and permitted.

## Fitness gates

| Dimension | Check |
|---|---|
| Identity | Genuine source resolves to stated title, author and version; no invented URLs/DOIs |
| Directness | Prefer relevant primary evidence; first-party docs fit own configuration claims |
| Accountability | Identifiable author/publisher, method/data traceable for the claim |
| Currency | Dates/version fit requested time; correction or retraction is not hidden |
| Independence | Count origin_id families; copies/syndication share the upstream origin |
| Context | Product, population, jurisdiction, language, definitions and period match |
| Conflict | Preserve credible opposing passages and disclose conflicts of interest |

Choose genuine relevant primary, international and Vietnamese sources by contextual fit,
not country rank. Vietnamese law/statistics require the applicable Vietnamese authority and
effective period; international comparison may use an appropriate international primary body.
Research findings need the actual paper/data and its methods; press releases and AI summaries
are leads, not independent proof. A first-party statement is not independent evidence of quality.

## Hard failures and conflicts

Fabricated/unresolvable identity, absent upstream origin, non-entailing passage, retracted
evidence presented as current, or mismatched scope block SUPPORTED. Find an accessible
alternative or label NEI. Paywall/access failure does not authorize citing unseen content.
Multiple URLs copied from one press release count as one origin, not corroboration.
Require independent corroboration when risk, volatility or dispute warrants it; one strong
primary source may suffice for a narrow low-risk exact specification.

When credible sources conflict, keep both paths; compare definitions, dates, authority and
scope. Resolve only with an explicit supported rationale; otherwise CONTESTED. URL counts
and aggregate quality scores cannot cancel fatal flags or settle semantic disagreements.
Structural validators check recorded provenance, not semantic proof; reviewer acceptance
records a judgment that can be challenged by counter-evidence.
